**Proposed Implementation Plan for Oracare Fulfillment App Update**

• Repurpose existing Google Service Account for the Oracare fulfillment app.

• Migrate the Google Drive integration from the old connector to the repurposed Service Account.

**Key Goals**

• **Leverage existing infrastructure:** Utilize a proven, working service account for Google Drive access.

• **Ensure seamless XML uploads:** Verify orders are uploaded from Google Drive into the fulfillment app database.

• **Transition safely:** Disable the old process only after successful integration and testing.

**Implementation Steps**

• **Identify Existing Service Account:** Locate the Google Service Account currently used by the old process for XML data uploads.

• **Assess and Adjust Permissions:** Verify the existing service account has the necessary, and minimal, permissions for the Oracare Fulfillment App's Google Drive integration. Adjust if needed to adhere to the principle of least privilege.

• **Configure Fulfillment App:** Update the Oracare Fulfillment App to use the identified and verified Google Service Account for Google Drive integration.

• **Develop & Test XML Upload:** Implement and thoroughly test the XML data upload functionality from Google Drive into the fulfillment app's database using the repurposed service account. This includes unit, integration, and performance testing.

• **Monitor & Verify in Staging:** Closely monitor the new integration in a staging environment to ensure stability and correct data flow.

• **Create Rollback Plan:** Document a clear rollback strategy in case unforeseen issues arise during the transition.

• **Safely Disable Old Process:** Once the new integration is fully functional and stable in production, safely disable the old process that was handling XML uploads.

• **Update Documentation:** Revise `NOTE-1662` (tech-doc) to reflect the new implementation strategy and service account usage.
